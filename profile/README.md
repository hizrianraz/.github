# Hizrian Raz

**Super intelligence economy scarcity research.** Bali.

I study how a physical limit becomes a price in the economy being built around artificial
intelligence. Four scarce inputs, each treated as its own market, on its own clock:

- **Compute** — an hour of GPU time. The base unit, and one without a long price history.
- **Memory** — DRAM and high-bandwidth memory. Capacity is settled in silicon years
  before anyone asks for it.
- **Metals** — copper first, then the other conductors in the build-out.
- **Energy & Power** — fuel at the wellhead, power at the node. A megawatt-hour is used
  where it is made, and then it is gone.

> Scarcity is given. Price is an argument.

A limit is written in a rack, a die, a mine or a grid. The price is not — it is an
argument about that limit, and the argument is what I write about.

## How I work

- **Written first.** A change of mind is a new note, not a quiet rewrite.
- **Named sources.** If a number is a proxy, it is called a proxy. If something is
  unconfirmed, it says so.
- **Negative results count.** A study that fails is written up as a study that failed.
- **Separate markets stay separate.** Four books, four clocks, no blended story.
